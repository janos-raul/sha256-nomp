    How the password parameters work

    • Put the parameters in your miner's password field, separated by commas. The order doesn't matter, and anything not set uses the pool default.
    • They are read when your miner logs in. After changing them, restart or reconnect the miner.
    • tt=, rt= and vp= are always kept within the allowed range, so they can't be misconfigured into something harmful. At worst your difficulty adjusts less smoothly.
    • d= sets where your difficulty starts; md= is a floor that VarDiff never goes below.
